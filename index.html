Account Deletion Instructions

We are sorry to see you go. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow these steps from within the app:

  1. Open the app on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to your account Settings or Profile section.
  3. Locate and tap the button labeled Delete my account.
  4. Confirm your decision when prompted.

Please note: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once confirmed, all your personal data, including your game progress and unlocked classes, will be securely and permanently removed from our servers.
